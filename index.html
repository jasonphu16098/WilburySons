Welcome To Moorabbin

A Warm Homage to 1970s Music Culture & Great Hospitality.

Wilbury & Sons is Moorabbin's go-to neighbourhood bar and kitchen. Inspired by classic recording studios of the 70s—warm timber, plush leather, cozy lighting, and genuine vinyl sound—we're built on three simple principles: exceptional drinks, soul-nourishing food, and heartfelt live music.

Located right in the heart of Station Street, whether you're dropping in for a knock-off pint after work, sharing tapas plates with old friends, or catching the finest local songwriters, Wilbury feels like home.
